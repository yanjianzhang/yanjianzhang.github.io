I’m Yanjian Zhang, a joint Ph.D. candidate at Université Sorbonne Paris Nord and Université Paris Cité, affiliated with LIPN and LLF. My research focuses on LLM reasoning, world modeling, and research-reproduction agents.
Previously, I received an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Degree (EMJMD) in
Big Data Management and Analytics, with a full Erasmus Mundus scholarship, and my bachelor’s degree from the School of Data Science, Fudan University. \
